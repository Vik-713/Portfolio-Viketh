About Me

Learning by building

I'm a Computer Science student who learns best by building. Over the last couple of years I've moved through frontend development, backend services, databases, APIs, and more recently, machine learning, real-time analytics, and IoT — usually by picking a problem and just trying to make it work.

Right now I'm getting increasingly interested in artificial intelligence, machine learning, and multi-agent systems — how independent AI agents can coordinate to solve real problems, and how AI can be applied to messy, real-world data instead of just clean textbook examples. I'm still figuring a lot of this out. That's kind of the point.
